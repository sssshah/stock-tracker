Privacy Policy

Stock Ticker Streamer  ·  Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Stock Ticker Streamer is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information about you.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Crash reports or diagnostics
  • Any information that could identify you personally

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following information is stored only on your Mac using macOS UserDefaults and never transmitted to us:

  • Your portfolio symbols (e.g. AAPL, MSFT)
  • Window position and size preferences

This data stays entirely on your device and is never shared with anyone.

Third-Party Data Sources

Stock Ticker Streamer fetches financial data from Finnhub.io. When the app requests stock quotes and news, your IP address is transmitted to Finnhub's servers as part of normal internet communication. Please review Finnhub's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle requests.

We do not share your portfolio symbols or any identifying information with Finnhub beyond what is technically required to fetch financial data.

No Advertising

Stock Ticker Streamer contains no advertising and does not use any advertising frameworks or tracking SDKs.

No Third-Party Analytics

The app does not include any analytics frameworks (such as Firebase, Mixpanel, or similar services).

Children's Privacy

Stock Ticker Streamer does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
